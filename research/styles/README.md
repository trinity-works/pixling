# Style search (2026-10-07)

How the launch styles were found. Lab notes, kept as written; paths inside them point at the session scratchpad the
prototypes were built in. The result lives in [docs/STYLES.md](../../docs/STYLES.md).

| Step | What happened | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Six mood images (Scenario), each turned into a palette and rendered on vista's Highgate kit. Verdict: a palette gives a season or a biome, never a new direction; the painters' hand is what differs. | `round1_FINDINGS.md`, `img/round1_compare.jpg` |
| Round 2 | Fifteen more refs plus the user's picks; a DNA sheet per direction (camera, shapes, shading, edges, shadows, marks, palette, vocabulary) and the shared taste layer. | `round2_DNA.md`, `SHARED_LAYER.md` |
| Round 3 | The maybes in three biomes each, same hand: Saffron, Marsh, Lantern Dither (became the optional night dither), an authored Tactics. | `round3_NOTES.md`, `img/round3_board.jpg` |
| Prototypes | Frost and Flat v1 (SDF + painted map), Flat v2 (exact iso, clean stairs), Saffron (ink line), Marsh (clusters), Tactics v2 (exact iso, soft line). | `proto_*.md`, `img/` |

Votes: Vista, Crisp Tactics and Flat Minimal launch. Saffron and Marsh are parked. Frost becomes vista's winter.
Etching, Toy Diorama and Blossom Light were dropped; Lantern Dither ships as an option, not a style.

Third-party references (the user's Pinterest picks) were mood only. They are not in this folder and never go in a
gallery; the generated refs are not shipped either.
