How it works

01 · ask

Say what your game needs

A town, a creature, a spell, a whole new look. In your words, in the chat you already use.

02 · choose

It picks a pipeline and a look

Before it draws, your agent reads two things: the pipeline that fits the job, with the lessons we learned the hard way, and the style's making-of, with its hand, its kit and its lab notes.

spec → spriteskit → iso sceneliving mapseffectsAI video → framesBlender → painterlynew style
03 · build

It programs the art

No image model here. Your agent writes shapes on bones, materials from the style and the timing of each frame, and pixling renders them pixel by pixel.

04 · look

It looks, like an artist would

It renders, then looks: up close at the pixels, then zoomed out at game scale beside the rest of the set. It names the one thing that doesn't read, fixes it with the style's own colours and renders again, until it holds up. Lints only hint; the eye decides.

renderlook up closelook at game zoomone fixagain
05 · ship

Game-ready, layer by layer

Sprite sheets, animation timing, collision, depth and lighting for every hour, straight into your repo.
